# Introduction

I am working as a Cyber Security Analyst and, inspired by [Malware-Traffic-Analysis.net],
I decided to start this blog of my own in order to analyse malicious email attachments and
links that I receive on my public email address and, in doing so, hone my skills. The blog
will be updated whenever I receive a malicious email and have the time to analyse it.
